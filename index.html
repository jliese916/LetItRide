Let It Ride Decision Guide

Let a removable bet ride when the visible cards match a Ride rule below. In an indifferent state, pulling and riding are equally optimal.

s = number of distinct five-card straights that can contain all visible ranks  ·  h = number of visible cards ranked ten or higher

First decision · three cards

Decide whether the middle bet stays in action.

RIDE Made hands

Three of a kindAny rank.
Pair of tens or betterTens, jacks, queens, kings, or aces.

RIDE Three unpaired suited cards

All three cards must share a suit.
s + h ≥ 3

PULL All other hands

Pull back the middle bet when the hand does not fit either Ride category above.

Decide whether the final removable bet stays in action.

RIDE Guaranteed payout

Pair of tens or betterThe fifth card cannot remove the paying pair.
Two pair, trips, or quadsAlready guaranteed to pay.

RIDE Four-card flush

Any four visible cards of the same suit.

RIDE Four-card straight draw

4s + h > 8

EITHER Indifferent straight draw

4s + h = 8

PULL All other hands

Pull back the bet when the hand does not fit a Ride or indifferent category above.
